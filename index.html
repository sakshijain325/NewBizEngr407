How it works
STEP 1

Describe your life

Home type, hours away, activity, allergies, kids and other pets, and a realistic monthly budget.

STEP 2

See ranked matches

Each animal gets a fit score with plain-language reasons and open questions, so nothing surprises you after adoption.

STEP 3

Send a ready profile

One tap shares your completed profile with the shelter or breeder. They skip the back-and-forth, you hear back faster.

Why breeders here are different

Every breeder is checked in person before they can list.

Many free listing sites call every breeder ethical. We verify before anyone lists, and re-verify every year. Shelters and rescues are confirmed as registered nonprofits or municipal facilities.
